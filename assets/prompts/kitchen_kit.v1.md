## 3. Asset

A parts kit ("kit of separate cut-paper pieces") for a small student apartment kitchen, to be assembled in code into a miniature paper diorama set. It is NOT a scene: every piece stands alone on the empty background, with nothing arranged into a kitchen. Two images are attached. The first is the style tile: copy its torn-paper cardstock look, brushwork, paper grain and the thin pale torn fibre rim exactly. The second is the frozen character sheet of the gator student, for the same line weight, rim, flat colour and picture-book simplicity; do NOT draw the gator.

Every piece is a flat orthographic cut-out, like a piece of a theatre set seen straight on, with no perspective, no 3D top faces and no depth: front elevation. Flat simple geometric shapes, strong silhouettes that would read if filled solid black, no clutter, no tiny detail. Cardstock, kraft card and cream tissue only. Palette: night navy, cream, gator green (small accents only), kraft brown, a few touches of lamp amber. No pink, no purple, no magenta, no red.

Make each one unmistakable by its signature features: the fridge is tall and rounded with a long vertical handle and a small freezer line, the stove has four round burners and knobs and a window in its oven door, the sink has a faucet with a curved neck and two taps.

Layout: one sheet, landscape, four rows, the pieces evenly spaced, nothing touching or overlapping, every piece at least 60 px from every other piece and from the frame edges. Each piece is one closed shape (or the exact set of parts named). Within each piece, the proportions below (width : height) are exact. The pieces may be drawn at different sizes on the sheet so each is as large as the sheet allows, but keep the big appliances in the same relative order of size (fridge, upper cabinets, stove, counter, sink, microwave, then the small props).
