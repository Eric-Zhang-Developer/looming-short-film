## 3. Asset

A parts kit ("kit of separate cut-paper pieces") for a university exam room, to be assembled in code into a miniature paper diorama set. It is NOT a scene: every piece stands alone on the empty background, nothing arranged into a room. Every piece is a flat orthographic cut-out, like a piece of a theatre set seen straight on, with no perspective, no 3D top faces and no depth: side or front elevation as stated. Flat simple geometric shapes, strong silhouettes that would read if filled solid black, no clutter, no tiny detail. Cardstock, kraft card and cream tissue only.
