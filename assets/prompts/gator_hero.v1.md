## 3. Asset

A single large character portrait of "the Student," the film's hero: a young alligator college student made as a cut-paper puppet. Two images are attached. The first is the style tile (the look of the whole film). The second is his approved character sheet. Paint only the full side-view figure from the top left of that sheet, exactly as it is there, much larger, alone on the image. Same pose, same design, same shapes, colours and proportions. Do not redesign him.

The figure, as on the sheet: standing in side view facing right, arms hanging down at his sides with the near mitten hand just below the hoodie hem, legs straight, feet flat. Flat simple geometric shapes like a mid-century picture-book illustration, with a strong silhouette. A tall rounded head running forward into a long straight blunt snout with a squared-off rounded tip, held level, with one tiny dark nostril dot near the tip. A semicircle eye bump on top of the head with one big round eye in it: a cream circle with a large black pupil looking forward and one small cream dot in the pupil. Green upper jaw, cream lower jaw, a gently upturned smile line where they meet, with two to four tiny cream triangle tooth tips peeking up along it, friendly and neat. A boxy oversized cream hoodie from neck to hips, the hood down as a soft rolled collar at the back of the neck, straight sleeves with rounded cuffs, round green mitten hands. Two short stubby green legs, about one fifth of his standing height, with small feet pointing forward. Behind him a long tapering tail with a blunt rounded tip, a cream underside and a single row of three or four small rounded scallops in a slightly darker green along its top edge.

Size and framing: the figure is large. From the top of the eye bump to the bottom of the feet he fills about 85 percent of the image height, centred, with at least 60 px of empty background above, below, and on the left and right of the whole figure, tail and snout tip included. Nothing touches or is cropped by the frame edge. Only this one figure is on the image: no other pieces, no parts, no turnaround.

The paper: he is torn paper, not felt. Each shape is flat colour cut from matte card, with dry-brush gouache texture and fine paper grain inside, and a visible pale torn fibre rim along every cut edge, 3 to 8 pixels wide at this size, exactly like the torn edges of the navy wall and the brown desk in the style tile: a thin irregular band of paler, whiter exposed paper core where the card was torn, slightly uneven in width. The rim is a little wider and raggeder on the tissue-weight cream hoodie than on the green card. The overlapping pieces (head over hoodie collar, arm over hoodie, hoodie over legs) each show their own torn rim where they overlap. No felt, no fuzz, no pile, no hairs or fibres sticking out past the edge. The hoodie is plain cream paper with no fold shading. Nothing is drawn as a 3D object with a top and a side.

Palette: gator green #6F8F5E, a slightly darker green only for the tail scallops, cream #F2E8D5 for the hoodie, belly, lower jaw, tooth tips, eye and torn rims, black only for the pupil and the nostril dot. Nothing in the artwork is magenta, pink or purple. No navy, no amber, no kraft.

Banned: no text, letters, numbers or labels. No eyebrow, no brow ridge, no scales, no spikes, no claws, no tongue, no gums. No cast shadows, no drop shadows, no outline glow, no ground line, no figure lighter on one side than the other.

Size: the largest landscape size available.
