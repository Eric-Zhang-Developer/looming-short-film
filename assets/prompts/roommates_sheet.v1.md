## 3. Asset

A parts sheet for two supporting characters, "the Roommates", as cut-paper puppets. Two images are attached. The first is the style tile (the look of the whole film: copy its torn-paper cardstock look, brushwork, paper grain and the thin pale torn fibre rim exactly). The second is the frozen character sheet of "the Student", the film's hero alligator: copy its house style exactly (flat simple geometric shapes like a mid-century picture-book illustration, strong silhouette, torn paper not felt, the same line weight, the same pale torn rim 2 to 6 px wide, fine paper grain), and keep every new character a different animal from him and at the same level of simplicity. Do NOT draw the Student or any gator unless a piece below says so.

The two roommates are two different animals, both muted and desaturated so they never steal focus from the hero: dusty, quiet colours, nothing bright. The TALL roommate is a lanky heron: a long thin neck, a long straight dagger beak, a narrow body in a loose, slouchy slate-blue-grey sweater (a desaturated mix of night navy and cream), cream-grey feathers on the head and belly, thin stick legs. The ROUND roommate is a plump bear cub: a soft round kraft-brown body in a faded cream t-shirt, a round head with two small round ears, short snout, relaxed and sleepy. Both are friendly and a bit oblivious, both seen in side view facing the viewer's right.
