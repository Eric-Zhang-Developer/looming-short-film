## 3. Asset

A parts kit ("kit of separate cut-paper pieces") for the outside of a long modernist university library at night, to be assembled in code into a miniature paper diorama. It is NOT a scene: every piece stands alone on the empty background. Copy the style tile's torn-paper cardstock look, brushwork, paper grain and thin pale torn fibre rim, and the home kit's flat, simple, picture-book style.

Every piece is a flat orthographic cut-out in front elevation (like a piece of a theatre set seen straight on), no perspective, no 3D side faces, no depth, except plan-free strips where stated. Simple geometric shapes, strong silhouettes. Cardstock, kraft card and cream tissue only.

Palette: brick is a dark terracotta kraft, close to night navy (kraft darkened with navy); dark bronze bands are a darker kraft-brown; lit windows are warm cream tissue; the night sky is night navy; tree canopy is a dark navy-green (gator green darkened with navy); lantern panels are lamp amber-yellow; Century Tower is pale cream stone. No pink, purple, magenta, red, bright orange or bright blue. No school colours.

Layout: one sheet, landscape, four rows, pieces evenly spaced, nothing touching or overlapping, every piece well clear of the others and of the frame edges. Within each piece the proportions (width : height) are exact. Pieces may be drawn at different sizes so each is as large as the sheet allows.
