## 3. Asset

A kit of flat wall clock pieces for a paper diorama, seen straight on, as the pieces of a cutout puppet. Three images are attached: the style tile (the look of the whole film); a sheet of props whose wall calendar (cream paper, navy header band, kraft hanging tab, torn paper rim) shows the paper style to match; and a set sheet whose round wall clock (a navy rim around a cream face with navy tick marks, two navy hands and an amber centre pin) shows the clock shape to match. Do not copy the attached sheets' other pieces. Paint these seven pieces, evenly spaced on one landscape canvas in two rows, nothing touching or overlapping, every piece at least 60 px from every other piece and from the frame edge:

Top row, left to right:
(1) HOME CLOCK FACE: the whole wall clock face as one piece, large, a perfect circle. A kraft brown (#A8835A) rim about one eighth of the radius wide around a cream (#F2E8D5) face. On the face, twelve tick marks in dark navy (#1E2440), short thick rounded bars pointing at the centre, the four at 12, 3, 6 and 9 o'clock longer and thicker than the other eight. NO NUMBERS, no letters, no brand, no logo. No hands and no centre pin: leave the face empty in the middle, because the hands are separate pieces.
(2) EXAM ROOM CLOCK FACE: the same clock face at exactly the same size, the same cream face and the same navy tick marks and tick sizes, no hands, no numbers, no centre pin, but with a navy (#1E2440) rim instead of kraft brown, a slightly cooler, plainer school-wall look.
(3) HOUR HAND: one navy hand, short and wide, a straight rounded bar with a rounded tip, drawn pointing straight up (toward 12) with a small round hole-free tail extending a little below its pivot end. Its pivot end (the end nearest the centre) sits about one quarter of its length above the bottom of the piece. About 30 percent of the face's diameter long.
(4) MINUTE HAND: one navy hand, the same style and width as the hour hand but longer and a little thinner, a straight rounded bar pointing straight up, with the same small tail. About 42 percent of the face's diameter long.
(5) CENTRE PIN: a small round amber (#F0A84A) disc with a slightly darker rim, about one twelfth of the face's diameter, seen straight on.

Bottom row, left to right, the two pieces that sell the paper look:
(6) HANGING TAB: a small kraft brown paper tab with a hole, like the one at the top of the calendar, about one fifth of the face's diameter wide, to hang the home clock.
(7) SPARE HAND: a second minute hand, the same as (4), pointing straight up, so the film has a spare to cut. 

Size: the face pieces (1) and (2) are each about 45 percent of the canvas height across. The hands and pin are drawn at the same scale as the faces. Every piece is one complete closed shape; the hands are separate pieces, never touching the faces.

The paper: every piece is torn cut paper, flat colour on matte card, with dry-brush gouache texture and fine paper grain, and a thin, pale, irregular torn fibre rim along every cut edge, like the calendar and the style tile. No felt, no fuzz, no pile. Flat, not 3D: no bevel, no glass cover, no reflection, no shine, no inner shadow.

Palette: kraft brown #A8835A, cream #F2E8D5, night navy #1E2440, lamp amber #F0A84A only on the centre pin. Nothing in the artwork is green, magenta, red or pink.

Banned: no text, letters, numbers, labels or brand marks anywhere, and no numerals or roman numerals on the faces. No second hand, no pendulum, no cast shadows, no drop shadows, no outline glow.
