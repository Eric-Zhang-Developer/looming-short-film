## 3. Asset

A sheet of six replacement heads for "the Student," a young alligator college student made as a cut-paper puppet. Two images are attached. The first is his hero portrait: look only at his HEAD, the green head with the eye bump, the long straight snout, the cream lower jaw, the one big eye, the nostril dot, the two tooth tips and the smile line. The second is the style tile.

Paint six copies of that exact head, side view facing right, each alone, arranged in a grid of three columns and two rows, evenly spaced, nothing touching or overlapping, at least 70 px of empty background between any two heads and around the outside of the sheet. Left to right, top row first: (1) numb, (2) dread, (3) resolve, (4) relief, (5) surprised, (6) pride. Do not write any labels.

IDENTITY IS THE POINT. All six are the same gator as the portrait and the same drawing as each other. Do not redesign him and do not redraw the head differently for each mood. Keep exactly, in all six: the outline of the head and the round eye bump on top; the long straight blunt snout held level with its squared-off rounded tip and the one small dark green nostril dot near the tip; the green upper jaw and the cream lower jaw, with the same cream throat swooping back and down behind where the hoodie collar would be; the same flat gator green #6F8F5E with dry-brush texture and fine paper grain; the same visible pale torn paper rim along every cut edge; the same size. All six heads are exactly the same size as each other, about 400 px wide from the back of the head to the snout tip on a 1536 px wide canvas. Only the face acts: the eye, the brow, the mouth and a few small marks change, and the head tilts not at all.

The head is drawn whole, the way it sits on the portrait, but with no hoodie. The back of the head and the throat continue downward past where the collar would be, about as far again as the eye is wide, and stop in a straight horizontal cut edge at the bottom, as if tucked behind a collar that is not drawn. The cream lower jaw and throat end at the same straight bottom edge. No hoodie, no body, no arms, no neck beyond that cut.

Shared drawing rules for the six faces: the eye sits in the eye bump exactly where the portrait's eye sits, a cream disc (the portrait's eye size, about 82 px across at this scale) unless the mood changes it. The pupil is black with one small cream highlight dot, as in the portrait. Brows are new: each brow is one thick dark green (#4A6640) strip of cut paper with rounded ends, drawn on the green of the head just above the eye, about as long as the eye is wide and about 1.3 times as thick as the smile line. Mouths and every other mark are the same dark green #4A6640 line, the same thickness as the portrait's smile line (about 1.4 percent of the head's width). Sweat drops are cream paper teardrops with the same torn rim. Every mark is flat cut paper or a flat painted line, simple and bold, so it reads when the head is small on a phone.

The six moods:

(1) numb: half-lidded and slack. The eye disc is covered from the top by a green lid down to about the middle of the disc, the lid edge a straight, slightly sleepy line with a thin dark green line along it; the pupil is small-ish and looks down and a little ahead, unfocused. One brow, flat and low, barely above the lid, tilted very slightly down toward the back of the head. The mouth is a plain, nearly straight dark green line with its front end sagging a little downward, no smile, no teeth showing. Nobody home.

(2) dread: worried. The eye disc is a little larger, the pupil smaller and looking down and back, with a thin slanted green lid sliver across the top of the disc. The brow is raised high above the eye and tilted: its snout-side (right) end is the high end and its back end is lower, so the brow is drawn up and in, the classic worried slant. One cream sweat drop sits on the green of the head just behind the eye, overlapping the green so it is part of the head, and a second smaller drop below it. The mouth is a short, tight, slightly wavy dark green line, pulled flat, no teeth showing.

(3) resolve: jaw set, eyes level. The eye is the full cream disc with the pupil looking straight ahead at the snout, calm and steady, no lid. The brow is flat and low, pressed close above the eye, level with the ground, slightly lower at its snout end. The mouth is a firm, perfectly straight dark green line, the lower jaw pressed up tight against the upper jaw, with no teeth showing. Determined and quiet, not angry.

(4) relief: eyes closed, a soft exhale. The eye is closed: no disc, only one thick dark green arch (an upside-down U) where the eye is, the same weight as the smile line. The brow is raised high above it in a soft gentle curve, lifted at both ends, as if the weight just came off. The mouth is a very faint, small smile, flatter than the portrait's, with the mouth slightly parted: a tiny gap of darker green at its front end. A small cream puff of breath, a soft rounded cloud shape of two or three bumps with the torn rim, floats just in front of the snout tip, touching the tip so that it is one piece with the head. The head sits a little relaxed, tired and happy.

(5) surprised smile: eyes wide, a small smile. The eye is the full cream disc a bit larger than in the portrait with a bright round pupil, a little smaller than the portrait's, centred looking ahead, with its highlight dot, wide open and delighted, no lid. The brow is raised in a soft high arch above the eye. The mouth is the portrait's own gentle upturned smile line, small, with the two tooth tips peeking up along it. Pleasantly startled, a little shy.

(6) pride: a big grin, eyes bright. The eye is the full cream disc with a big black pupil looking a little upward and forward, and a larger bright highlight dot, plus a second tiny highlight dot, open and shining. The brow is raised in a soft relaxed arch. The mouth is a big wide grin: the smile line is long, curving up strongly toward the snout tip, with four or five small cream tooth tips along it, and at its back end a small round upturned cheek-corner mark (a short dark green curved dash). Happy and proud, not manic.

Palette: gator green #6F8F5E for the head, cream #F2E8D5 for the lower jaw, throat, eye discs, tooth tips, sweat drops, breath puff and torn rims, dark green #4A6640 for every line, brow and the nostril dot, black only for pupils. Nothing in the artwork is magenta, pink, purple, red, blue, amber, navy or kraft.

Banned: no text, letters, numbers or labels anywhere. No eyelashes, no cheeks or blush, no tongue, no gums, no lips, no scales, no spikes. No hoodie. No cast shadows, no drop shadows, no outline glow, no highlights or 3D shine except the pupils' dots, no ground line, no head lighter on one side than the other, no head tilted or rotated. No felt, no fuzz, no pile, no hairs sticking out past the edge.

Size: the largest landscape canvas available (about 1536 × 1024 px).
