## Assembled prompt (piped on stdin)

## 1. Style

Hand-painted cut-paper illustration for a stop-motion paper diorama. Matte cardstock surfaces painted with dry-brush gouache: visible bristle streaks, slightly uneven pigment, soft paper grain. Limited palette only: night navy #1E2440, cream #F2E8D5, gator green #6F8F5E, kraft brown #A8835A, plus small touches of lamp amber #F0A84A. Picture-book charm, simple rounded shapes, strong readable silhouettes. Match the attached style tile exactly in brushwork, texture and color.

## 2. Lighting

Flat, even, shadowless studio lighting, as if the paper were scanned. No cast shadows, no drop shadows, no highlights, no rim light, no glow, no vignette, no ambient occlusion. Lighting will be added later by a 3D engine, so the art must contain none.

## 3. Asset

The style tile for the film: a small corner of a student's apartment at night, built as a paper diorama and shown as its separate cut-paper pieces, straight-on front view. The attached image is a reference frame from a torn-paper picture-book animation; match its torn-paper feel, not its content.

Pieces, each a complete closed cut-paper shape with a slightly torn edge and a thin pale fiber rim:
(1) a section of wall, night navy cardstock with dry-brush texture;
(2) a desk edge, kraft brown card with visible fiber texture;
(3) a paper wall calendar, cream pages, a grid of blank boxes with one day circled in red (the only red in the image);
(4) a desk lamp with a translucent cream tissue shade with a ragged edge, painted flat with no glow;
(5) a closed laptop, night navy and cream.

Layout, left to right, evenly spaced, nothing touching or overlapping: every piece sits apart with about 40 px of empty background around it, so each can be cut out separately. No piece is cropped by the frame edge.

Background key: green #00FF00. Nothing in the artwork is green. For this image the palette has no gator green: use only night navy, cream, kraft brown, small touches of lamp amber, and the single red circle.

Banned: no text, no letters, no numbers on the calendar (its boxes stay blank), no logo on the laptop.

Size: the largest landscape size available.

## 4. Technical

Background: fully transparent (real alpha channel), with clean edges and no halo. If transparency isn't possible, use perfectly flat solid pure green #00FF00 instead, with no gradient, no texture and no shadow on it, and no green anywhere in the artwork. Highest available resolution, landscape. No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks or borders anywhere.

## Output

Generate exactly one image. Save it as `out.png` in the current working directory (the workspace root).
