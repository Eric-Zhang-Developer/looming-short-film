## Assembled prompt (piped on stdin)

## 1. Style

Hand-painted cut-paper illustration for a stop-motion paper diorama. Matte cardstock surfaces painted with dry-brush gouache: visible bristle streaks, slightly uneven pigment, soft paper grain. Limited palette only: night navy #1E2440, cream #F2E8D5, gator green #6F8F5E, kraft brown #A8835A, plus small touches of lamp amber #F0A84A. Picture-book charm, simple rounded shapes, strong readable silhouettes. Match the attached style tile exactly in brushwork, texture and color.

## 2. Lighting

Flat, even, shadowless studio lighting, as if the paper were scanned. No cast shadows, no drop shadows, no highlights, no rim light, no glow, no vignette, no ambient occlusion. Lighting will be added later by a 3D engine, so the art must contain none.

## 3. Asset

The style tile for the film: a small corner of a student's apartment at night, built as a paper diorama and shown as its separate cut-paper pieces laid out flat, straight-on front view. There is no style tile yet; this image becomes it. The attached image is a reference frame from a torn-paper picture-book animation: match its torn-paper feel, its dry-brush texture and its thin pale fiber rims along torn edges, not its content, characters or colors.

Six pieces. Each is one complete closed cut-paper shape with a slightly torn edge and a thin pale fiber rim. Vary the tear with the paper: heavier card tears tighter, the tissue shade tears more raggedly, so no two pieces share the same edge.
(1) a section of wall: night navy cardstock with dry-brush texture, plain, with nothing pinned or painted on it (no window, no notes, no skyline);
(2) a desk edge: kraft brown textured card with visible fiber, the desktop and front edge as one piece;
(3) a paper wall calendar: cream pages, a grid of blank boxes, exactly one box circled in calendar red (the only red in the image);
(4) a desk lamp with a translucent cream tissue shade with a ragged edge and a kraft or navy stem and base, painted flat with no glow;
(5) a closed laptop, night navy and cream, no logo;
(6) one small paper gator figure, the film's main character: a young alligator student, standing, in strict side view, facing right. Chunky rounded silhouette that reads at small size, short wide snout, one small round eye, a gentle slightly sleepy look. Gator green #6F8F5E cardstock body with a lighter cream belly and a darker dry-brush texture along the back and tail. Wears an oversized cream hoodie. In the diorama it is about 8 cm tall: draw it to scale with the desk edge, so if it stood beside the desk its head would come a little above the desktop.

Layout, left to right in one row, evenly spaced, nothing touching or overlapping: every piece sits apart with about 40 px of empty background around it, so each can be cut out separately. No piece is cropped by the frame edge. All pieces share one consistent scale.

Gator green appears only on the gator. Elsewhere use only night navy, cream, kraft brown, small touches of lamp amber, and the single red circle. Nothing in the artwork is magenta.

Banned: no text, no letters, no numbers on the calendar (its boxes stay blank), no logo on the laptop. No cast shadows, no drop shadows, no glow or halo around the lamp or anything else, and no baked-in light direction: no piece is lighter on one side than the other, every surface is lit evenly as if scanned.

Size: the largest landscape size available.

## 4. Technical

Background: fully transparent (real alpha channel), with clean edges and no halo. If transparency isn't possible, use perfectly flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF instead, with no gradient, no texture and no shadow on it, and no magenta anywhere in the artwork. Highest available resolution, landscape. No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks or borders anywhere.

## Output

Generate exactly one image. Save it as `out.png` in the current working directory (the workspace root).
