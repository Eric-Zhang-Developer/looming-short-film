v7-laptop · the blob's wall shadow, v6 against v7, at the same film times.
Measured from the live shots by evidence/v7-laptop/shadows.mjs (one headless Chrome): each shot is built as film.js builds it, updated to the time,
and its key light and Exam are read back. units = S · r / 0.012 with S = z_L / (z_L − z_B) (README §1). "width" projects the blob card's body
circle (radius r, r above its contact, through the mesh's world matrix) from the key's world position onto the wall (z 0) and takes its x extent,
in units of the 1× blob's diameter (0.024). v6 = this branch's base, 898603c (s02–s06 unchanged since the v6 render); v7 = this branch.
Film 12 is s02's calendar insert: the laptop is shut and the key is the window (directional, 1×), so there is no screen shadow to measure.

Every width is unchanged (limit ±5%). The light keeps its depth in every shot, and on a flat card S depends only on depth, so the size holds
exactly; what moves is the light's height and x (the bigger screen sits higher), and the shadow moves (1 − S) times that across the wall:
s03 1.35 cm lower; s06 2.2 cm right and 1.25 cm lower (its laptop is also 1 cm further left); s05 not at all (its laptop sinks into a thinner
cushion so the screen centre stays on v6's L).
