### 3. The dark blob and its shadow
**What I think it is:** dread or anxiety about the exam. It's born on the calendar date (0:14), and every student has one (1:58, 2:40). Not hostile so much as a heavy companion with moody eyes.

**Size of the blob itself:** tiny at 0:14 → head-sized while he avoids work (0:16–1:06) → torso-sized on the walk (1:22–1:34) → biggest, almost his height, at the library door (1:44) → still big at the desk (1:50–2:06) → shrinking as he solves problems (2:16–2:22) → tiny after the exam (2:56–3:00) → tiny again for the Final (3:08), with a dashed outline beside it. **Grows with:** the deadline plus avoidance. **Shrinks with:** actually doing problems, not the breath at the door (it stays big at the desk for about 15 s after it).

**The shadow follows a consistent rule** that also works physically: **a low, near light makes a giant wall or ceiling shadow; an overhead or warm lamp makes a small one.**
- Huge with a low, cold light: the phone (0:20–0:24, 0:44–0:46), the fridge (0:26–0:32), the laptop (0:34–0:38), under the covers (0:48–1:06), the low lamp (1:14–1:16).
- Small or gone with a warm or overhead light: the desk lamp (0:40–0:42, no shadow), the streetlamps (1:22–1:34, a ground puddle), the library lamps (2:02–2:06, a small desk shadow).
- Always has eye-holes, even on the desk. Not physical, but consistent.

Thematically: screen light means a monster-sized shadow, honest light a normal one. A cold viewer will likely feel this without being able to state it. The 0:40 lamp scene could just as easily read as "a different time of night".
