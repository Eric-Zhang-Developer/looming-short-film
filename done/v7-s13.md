## Story
The first surprise came before any code: the worktree had been cut from `main`, and my brief didn't exist in it. The brief was on `wave/3-assets`, 241 commits ahead. The branch was fresh and empty, so I reset it there and started over.

Then I looked at v3. I pulled its s13 frames and the audio's spectrogram off the Seagate drive, and the onsets measured 97.40, 98.40 and 99.40: exactly the wakes, 1.4, 2.4 and 3.4 s into the shot. So v3's sync was real, but it held only because the same number was typed into two files, and the seven generic wakes had no notes at all. That's why the first commit (`683a82b`) is a table, `S13_DESKS`, which the picture draws from and the score refuses to load without.

The first full sheet worked almost as written. It showed one real flaw: the last loom finished growing on the same frame the first phone went down, so the room was never full and still. Moving the featured wakes a quarter-second earlier bought that beat (`sheet.jpg`, 4.75–5.0).

The music needed one rethink. My `timing.mjs` measures each event's onset in the stem, and the rising E7 arpeggio for the room's wakes barely registered (+0.3 dB). It was sinking into the E7 chord it was spelling out. Moving it to the music box, the blobs' own voice, gave +3 to +6 dB, and that reads truer anyway: every one of those shadows is a blob speaking.

The last fix came from rereading the brief's rule, "never a big shadow without a lit phone". My collapses kept a shrinking loom for six frames after the light went out, so now the phone's glow dies with its shadow.
