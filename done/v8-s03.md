## Story
The brief was mostly about timing, so I started by asking what had to stay still: the light, the blob's sizes, and so the shadow's numbers. I ported v7-laptop's shadow harness to the compiled `build/` the TypeScript pass introduced, and measured v7 first by checking the old `s03.ts` back out over my edits. It gave 3.600 and 5.400 units, the same as `evidence/v7-laptop/shadows.txt`. That gave me a baseline that didn't depend on my memory of v7.

The old insert framing showed only the top-right corner of the laptop screen, which is right for a notification and wrong for an integral, so `reminderInsert` gained a centred `page` framing. I drew the banner into the practice page itself rather than on the insert's own card, so the wide shot's tiny screen carries it too when we cut back for the flinch.

The acting kit nearly tripped me. A flinch at 5.0 and numb for the game can't both land within half a second of each other (MIN_HOLD), so numb comes in at 6.0, once the spray has settled. For the muzzle I read the AK's single taps off an amplitude envelope (`0.36, 1.69, 2.64` s into the clip). My first wait-for-render loop never returned: its `pgrep` pattern matched its own shell.

Once rendered, the problem reads cleanly (`evidence/v8-s03/screen.png`). The second measure came back identical to v7 to the third decimal, with the swell landing exactly on 5.50 (`shadows_v8.txt`). The weak spot is the pen, which is too small at this framing to carry "pen up".
