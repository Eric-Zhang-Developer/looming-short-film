# Done · rig-legs

## What shipped
`gator_sheet` and `freshman_sheet` now have stubby `leg_l` / `leg_r` pieces (a proxy shin with a forward foot). The side rigs hang them from the hip, and the gator stands 8 cm. The root stays the hip, so s15's numbers don't move. `specPose` takes `leg` / `farLeg` world angles, and every gator and Freshman pose has legs: seated poses tuck them, kneels fold them back, standing poses plant them and prone poses lay them flat. The side walk has real heel/toe contacts and a new `walk_side_pass_b`. `gator.js` exports `HIP` and `hipHeight(pose)`, and `walk()` returns `hip`. The front and back turnarounds are 8 cm. Separately, the loader's `sizes` now puts the visible shape at the PIECES size, and it returns `visible` boxes. Rigs place parts by their visible box, so the proxy margin no longer shrinks them.

## Gaps
- **`lab/characters.js` isn't in my owns, and it still puts standing, kneeling and walking roots on the floor.** In the real lab page the legs hang through the floor, and the new `walk_side_pass_b` cell gets the seated chair. It needs `ROOT_Y.gator = HIP`, `ROOT_Y.freshman = HIP × 0.85`, `hipHeight(POSES[name])` for floor poses, `walk().hip` for the side set, `walk_side_pass_b` in `FLOOR`, and front/back `FRAME` heights about 0.095. `evidence/rig-legs/lab.js` is that change, ready to port.
- **Pose count is 93.** I added `walk_side_pass_b`; no existing name changed. A rigid one-piece leg can't clear the floor at a shared pass, because both legs would be plumb. The side walk is now a → pass → b → pass_b, and `walk()` picks `<set>_pass_b` when a set has one.
- **The walk's vertical rhythm differs from the classic.** Contact is the lowest drawing (hip 26.9 mm) and "up" the highest (30.2 mm), with no knee dip, because legs are one piece. The specs' "contact, down, pass, up" are still 4 distinct drawings per step. The stance foot rolls heel to toe, 0.024 per step, matching s18's step length.
- **Legs are longer than in the style tile.** Hip to sole is 37 % of his height, against roughly 20 % in `style_tile_v2_4`. That's forced: s15 fixes the head above the hip (top at +0.0505), and the brief fixes 8 cm. Shorter legs would mean a bigger head or a different eye height.

- **The proxy body draws 27.5 mm wide, not 30.** `makePaper` keeps the art's aspect, and the torso proxy is drawn at 0.9 of its box by design. The long axis is exact. The contract says this.
