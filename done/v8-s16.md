## Story
I started by reading s15 to see what s16 inherits, and the brief's first number didn't hold: the blob enters at 2.5×, not 2×. The beat sheet had copied a paragraph in the shot README that Q16 had overruled the day after it was written. Either way of following it literally breaks the film's one rule about size, so the relapse got its own half step to 3×. The same read showed the phone wasn't in his pocket. He had laid it face-down on the desk in s11, so he turns that one back up.

The first sheet (scratch, not kept) worked better than I expected. Before I placed anything, I computed the shadow from the phone the way s03 does from the laptop. It came up as a dome over the cubicle panel, between his desk and the neighbour's. But it was too dim, and both eyes landed in the window band, where the panes show the night and nothing reads. The fix came from the geometry: the eye holes' height on the wall depends only on how high the light sits. I tried putting the phone down at the desk instead of up at his face, and the eyes cleared the window. It also turned the pose into the doom-scroll hunch, with the screen lighting his chin from below.

The second pass's strip (`strip.jpg`) showed him too small in the wide to read his dread. So the reaction got a half-second of B′, and the wide comes back as the phone goes down. Now the shadow sinks with the phone and vanishes on the face-down frame, and you can watch it happen.
