## Gaps
- **The gator has no legs.** `gator_sheet` (assets.md, doc 03) has none, and s15 fixes shoulder (+0.030) and eye (+0.042) above the hip. That makes him **5.3 cm tall standing, not the 8 cm** in the contract Conventions and README §1 ("blob 30% of the 8 cm gator"). I sized the front and back turnarounds to 0.053 so s09's side-to-back swap holds. Walks are a legless waddle (body bob, arm and tail swing), and kneeling looks like standing. The specs talk about feet (s09 "between his feet", s18 "contact, down, pass, up"). **Director/G2 call:** add leg pieces to `gator_sheet`, or accept the waddle and correct the 8 cm figure.
- **Pose names follow the specs, not pure verbs,** where a spec already named one (`sit_screen`, `write_a`, `walk_side_a`, `top_head_down`). s11's `sit_upright` and s14's `sit_watch` are the same numbers, so both are `sit`. s14's `stoop_walk_a/b` became `walk_stoop_*` so `walk()` can find the set. Poses the specs didn't give numbers for (slump, shrug, s04 kneeling, s07 curtains, prone, crowd, roommates) are my estimates from the prose.
- **Borrowed parts:** `roommates_sheet` has no arms, but s05 has both roommates mash and cheer, so they use the Student's arm pieces, tinted. A painter could add arms to that sheet.
- **Art conventions are now contract** (tails point −x, arms horizontal with the shoulder end on the left, `turn_front` painted without arms). Proxy tapers point +x, so the rig mirrors proxy tails only. The cutter must follow these conventions or the first real tail will point forward.
- **Greens are mine.** `greenVariant(i)` mixes PALETTE.gator toward other palette colours. No spec gives crowd greens. The Freshman defaults to the hero's green.
- **The Exam:** `blob.js` knows eye modes `open`, `closed` and `worried` only. `tired`, `gasp` and `side`, which the specs use, silently draw `open`. That's a mod-blob gap; `exam.js` passes eyes through untouched. `scaleAt()` multiplies into the mesh scale, and blob.js's floor sync assumes an unscaled blob (its own ponytail note). Not checked on a scaled blob resting on a stain.
- **Not done:** a `follow('tail')` during `walk()` restarts its spring every cycle, so walk poses carry the tail swing instead (documented). The front view's head can't tilt: s10's head −12° has to be a face-slot nudge by the shot. Joint gaps at extreme angles weren't stress-tested beyond the poses themselves. Nothing was checked on frozen art (none exists).
- `buildRig` and `sideParts` are exported from `gator.js` for the sibling rig files only. They aren't in the contract and shots shouldn't call them.
- `tools/check_timeline.mjs` wasn't run because this task doesn't touch the timeline.
