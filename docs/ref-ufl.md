# UF reference brief: Marston, the Plaza, and the walk between

Design brief for the set painter. Images and licences: `reference-videos/ufl/` (gitignored), logged in `reference-videos/ufl/SOURCES.md`. Every claim is tagged **verified** (with the image or source) or **inferred**. Filenames below are in that folder.

## 0. Lighting at an upper-floor Marston desk

**Short answer: no source shows desk lamps on the upper floors, and none shows built-in task lights. The room is lit from overhead.**

- **Verified.** Floors 4 and 5 are silent-study floors with "great views" (marston.uflib.ufl.edu study-spaces and 4th-floor pages), with study nooks and charging areas (guides.uflib.ufl.edu/internationalstudents/uflibraries).
- **Verified.** The 2022 refit replaced the 1987 furniture, more than 90% of the project by the library's account, so students can run two or three devices at a table, and it added outlets (Alligator, 4 Jul 2022). That article says nothing about lamps or lighting, so it is silence, not proof of absence.
- **Verified.** A UF student essay calls Marston's lighting harsh fluorescent and says window spots are often taken (hercampus.com, "love letter to other libraries").
- **Verified (not Marston-confirmed).** In a UF library ceiling, fluorescent troughs sit between louvred panels (`uflib_int_03`). It is a UF library, floor unstated.
- **Verified.** Double-decker carrels belong to the Architecture and Fine Arts Library, not Marston (Alligator, Sep 2025). Do not draw carrels with built-in lights.
- **Not found.** Under-shelf lights, wall switches, motion sensors or timers, which outlets sit where, students bringing clip-on or USB lamps, lamp lending. Reddit and Yelp were not reachable, so student habits are unconfirmed.
- **Inferred.** Light comes from ceiling fixtures (fluorescent or LED) plus daylight; desks have outlets but no lamps; stack aisles likely sit on switched or timed lights, as is common (no Marston source); a student's own clip lamp is possible but unconfirmed.
- **Consequence for the film.** s15 and s18 hand a lamp to a desk that probably has none. It reads as something the hero carries in, or as a deliberate fantasy. The film's own rule that "the lamp comes from him" fits this. The director should decide which.

## 1. Marston interior, upper floors (hero desk, s10 to s17)

- **Gap.** No openly licensed photo of Marston's upper floors exists on Commons or Flickr. Everything here about the study floors is **inferred** from the building's shape, the atrium photos and other UF libraries.
- **Atrium (verified, `marston_int_01` to `03`).** A central court under a skylight. Cream concrete beams form a square grid. Stepped, bronze-brown bay boxes with window groups climb the sides, and brick walls sit behind. At night the bays glow warm cream against dark glass.
- **Window wall (inferred).** The exterior has continuous ribbon windows with thin mullions (verified, `marston_ext_02`, `_03`, `_07`). Upper-floor desks likely face these. They are wide and shallow, unlike the film's tall windows (0.10 m wide, sill 0.066, top 0.300), so choose one on purpose.
- **Out of the window at night (verified).** Oak canopy, black lampposts and the floodlit Century Tower (`marston_ext_08`, `tower_01`).
- **Shelving (verified, `uflib_int_01`, `_02`).** Dark steel uprights, shelves one spine deep, rows of colourful spines. Aisles recede into dark with a bright far end.
- **Lights and floors (inferred).** Fluorescent trough lights over the aisles. Floor finish unknown; draw a plain dark carpet or tile.
- **Palette.** Walls Night Navy. Pale cream window mullions and beams. Kraft shelves and desks. A faint green-cream tint on the fluorescent light. Amber only from the hero's lamp. Cyan only from phones.
- **Recognition cues.** The stepped bronze bays, the skylight grid, the shelves running into darkness. A wood door with a narrow vertical window (`uflib_int_04`) is verified for a UF library stairwell, probably Library West.

## 2. Marston exterior at night (s09)

- **Shapes (verified, `marston_ext_01`, `_02`, `_06`).** A long, blocky brick mass. Dark bronze horizontal bands run between brick slabs. Ribbon windows sit in the recesses. Upper levels step back in ziggurat bays. A tall open brick-pier breezeway cuts through the middle.
- **Courtyard (verified, `marston_ext_03`, `_04`).** Hexagonal brick planters, live oaks hung with moss, and a yellow structural sculpture nicknamed the French Fries. At dusk the ribbon windows glow in rows.
- **Lampposts (verified, `marston_ext_03`, `_07`).** Square lanterns with yellow panels on thin posts. Cluster versions hang under the bands. They turn to amber tissue.
- **Colour.** Brick becomes dark terracotta kraft, close to navy at night. Bands are darker kraft. Lit windows are cream. Skip the school's orange and blue.
- **Recognition cues.** The notch of breezeway with Century Tower in it (`marston_ext_05`), the yellow lanterns, the sculpture's sprawl, moss over the planters.

## 3. Plaza of the Americas and the walk

- **Shapes (verified, `plaza_01` to `_05`).** A broad lawn rimmed by big live oaks with Spanish moss, plus magnolias and pines. Pine-straw and leaf-litter ground. Concrete walks with a brick-inlay strip. Black ornamental lampposts with white globes. Black fluted bollards. Library West closes the north end: brick, a low orange tile roof, vertical fins (`plaza_05`, `plaza_06`).
- **Colour.** Canopy a mass of dark navy-green cardstock (reuse Gator Green, darkened). Pale cream globes. Kraft ground.
- **Cues.** Moss curtains, the bollard-and-globe lamppost, the brick strip across a pale walk.
- **Geography (verified from OpenStreetMap).** Plaza centre about 29.6505, -82.3427. Marston about 29.6480, -82.3440. Century Tower about 29.6488, -82.3433, almost on the line between them. A foot route is about 370 m, roughly 5 minutes (OSM foot router). Wikipedia places Marston beside Turlington, the University Auditorium and Century Tower. Walking south-west from the Plaza past the tower reaches Marston plausibly.

## 4. Leave out

All signage, building names, the UF logo, and the sign pictured in `marston_ext_05`. Bike racks, banners, posters, spine labels and call numbers. The "QUIET STUDY" plate. Any legible text. Crowds in the Plaza photos. School orange and blue.
