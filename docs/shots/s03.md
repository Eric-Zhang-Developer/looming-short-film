# Shot s03 · The laptop: the shadow climbs (rule, first time)

**Time:** 0:20–0:26 (6 s) · **Beats:** `laptopUp` @ 1.0 (global 0:21), `gulp1` @ 4.5 (global 0:24.5)
**Story job:** Instead of the practice sheet he opens his laptop to a game. The screen is a low, close light, so the blob's shadow climbs the wall at three times its size, and he doesn't notice. Then the blob gulps and grows a step: he looked at it and looked away (**the meter, first time**; README §1).

**G4 changes:** the phone became the laptop (director: "playing something on my laptop"; the phone stays for Marston). The light sits where the phone's grip spot was in depth (z 0.090), so the shadow scale and units are unchanged: S 3.0, 3.0 → 3.75 units. The shot is 6 s (was 5) to fit the lid opening.
