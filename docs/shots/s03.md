# Shot s03 · Phone up: the shadow climbs (rule, first time)

**Time:** 0:15–0:20 (5 s) · **Beats:** `phoneUp` @ 0.5 (global 0:15.5)
**Story job:** When he lights up his phone, low and close, the blob's shadow climbs the wall at three times its size, and he doesn't notice.

## Characters and action
**The Student.** On 2s.
- 0.00: carries in from s02: eyes `side` on the blob, mouth `line`.
- 0.00–0.17: anticipation. Shoulders sink 1.5 mm, head tilts −3°.
- 0.17–0.33: **the shrug.** Both arms rotate +20° at the shoulder, head tilts +6°, eyes `closed` at 0.17.
- 0.33–0.42: shoulders drop back. Eyes `tired` at 0.42, gaze down to the phone.
- **0.50 `phoneUp`:** left hand lifts the phone from rest to (0.000, 0.010, 0.235) and flips it screen-up, all on this one 2s frame. The screen wakes.
- 0.50–1.50: settles into a hunch, body leaning 8° forward. The phone eases (ease-out) down to the grip spot **L = (0.000, 0.003, 0.225)**.
- 1.50–5.00: hold the hunch. Thumb flicks (left hand +2° for one 2s frame) at 1.83, 2.33, 2.83, 3.33, 3.83, 4.33. Mouth `neutral` at 2.00. Eyes stay `tired` on the screen. **He never looks at the blob or the wall.**

**The Exam.** Wobble 1 throughout; the wobble makes the wall shadow breathe.
- 0.00–0.50: at B, eyes `open`, looking up at him.
- 0.58: eyes `side`, toward the glowing phone (curious).
- 2.50: eyes `side`, mirrored up-left: it glances at its own shadow on the wall. This leads the audience's eye to the rule.
- 3.33: eyes `open`, back to him.
- 3.50–3.67: scoots 5 mm toward him, to (−0.025, 0.012, 0.155): squash 0.2 for one frame, then a settle `spring(t, 3.67)`. Moving toward the light makes the shadow grow again (see Light).

## Light
- **Key: phone**, by the phone-rig convention used in every phone shot: the shadow light is `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, penumbra: 0.4, distance: 0.6 })` placed at the phone and aimed every frame along the phone→blob ray to its wall hit (`aim(spot, hit)`), so the cone holds the blob and leaves the Student out. The screen faces him, so a real screen would not light the blob; this is the deliberate shadow-play cheat (done note). His snout gets cyan from a separate unshadowed face fill: a `THREE.PointLight` in Phone Cyan at the phone + (0.005, 0.010, 0.005), `distance` 0.06, `decay` 2, `castShadow` false, about 15% of the spot's intensity.
- Phone spot intensity: 0 before 0.50, full from the 0.50 frame (light runs on 1s, so the ramp is a single frame, 0.50 → 0.54).
- **Blob shadow scale (receiver: wall z = 0):**

| Local t | Phone at | Blob at | Scale | Shadow on wall: center, y extent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 | (0.000, 0.010, 0.235) | B | **2.76×** | (−0.083, 0.016), y −0.018..0.049 |
| 1.50 | L (0.000, 0.003, 0.225) | B | **3.00×** | (−0.090, 0.030), y −0.006..0.066 |
| 3.67 | L | (−0.025, 0.012, 0.155) | **3.21×** | about (−0.08, 0.03) |

The shadow climbs from half-on-the-desk to above his head (head top y 0.05) between 0.5 and 1.5, then grows once more at 3.67. It lies over the calendar's lower edge.
- Off-axis checks from L: Student's body 131°, head 121°, lamp head 85°: all outside the 0.9 rad (52°) cone, so neither the Student nor the lamp throws a phone shadow on the wall. The blob's half-angle from L is 8°.
- **Morning window** (s02's `dawnLight`, same position, k 0.35) continues at 30% of its s02 intensity so the phone wins the wall. It still throws the faint 1.0× blob shadow back-left on the desk. Two shadows, and the big cyan-bounded one wins: doc 03 rule 6.
- **Fill:** `ambient()` default.
