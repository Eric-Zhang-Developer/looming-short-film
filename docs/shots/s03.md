# Shot s03 · Phone up: the shadow climbs (rule, first time)

**Time:** 0:15–0:20 (5 s) · **Beats:** `phoneUp` @ 0.5 (global 0:15.5)
**Story job:** When he lights up his phone, low and close, the blob's shadow climbs the wall at three times its size, and he doesn't notice. Then the blob gulps and grows a step: he looked at it and looked away (**the meter, first time**; README §1).

## Characters and action
**The Student.** On 2s. His near (camera-side) hand works the phone. Lean and arm angles as defined in s15 (lean + = forward toward +x; arm is the arm's world angle, 0 = +x).
- 0.00: carries in from s02: eyes `side` on the blob (and the calendar's circle behind it), mouth `line`, pose `slump`. **This is the look; the shrug and the phone are the look-away.**
- 0.00–0.17: anticipation. Shoulders sink 1.5 mm, head tilts −3°.
- 0.17–0.33: **the shrug.** Both arms rotate +20° at the shoulder, head tilts +6°, eyes `closed` at 0.17.
- 0.33–0.42: shoulders drop back. Eyes `tired` at 0.42, gaze down to the phone.
- **0.50 `phoneUp`:** the near hand lifts the phone from rest to (−0.015, 0.010, 0.105) and flips it screen-up, all on this one 2s frame. Fingertip on the phone's left end (−0.0225, 0.010): lean 16, arm −6. The screen wakes.
- 0.50–1.50: settles into a hunch. The phone eases (ease-out) down to the grip spot **L = (−0.012, 0.004, 0.090)**, fingertip at (−0.0195, 0.004): lean 24, arm −11 (both solved for ℓ 0.032 in `evidence/sb-reconcile/geometry.txt`).
- 1.50–5.00: hold the hunch. Thumb flicks (arm +2° for one 2s frame) at 1.83, 2.33, 2.83, 3.33, 3.83, 4.33. Mouth `neutral` at 2.00. Eyes stay `tired` on the screen. **He never looks at the blob or the wall.**

**The Exam** (`BLOB_R = { start: METER.birth, gulp1: METER.gulp1 }`: 1× → 1.25×). Wobble 1 throughout; the wobble makes the wall shadow breathe.
- 0.00–0.50: at B, eyes `open`, looking up at him.
- 0.58: eyes `side`, toward the glowing phone (curious).
- 2.50: eyes `side`, mirrored up-right: it glances at its own shadow on the wall. This leads the audience's eye to the rule.
- 3.33: eyes `open`, back to him.
- **3.50 `gulp1`: the first gulp** (README §1). 3.417 squash 0.3, eyes `closed`. **3.50 snaps to 1.25× (r 0.015)** in one drawing, stretched (squash −0.2), eyes `open` on him; `spring(t, 3.5)` settles by 3.75. Its contact point stays at B. Wobble 0.8 until 4.5. The wall shadow swells with it on the same frame. This replaces the old 3.50 scoot toward the phone: one growth idea at a time, and this one is the blob's.
- 4.00–5.00: holds at 1.25×, eyes `open` on him. He never looks.
