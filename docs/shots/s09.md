## Light
- **Key: the lit windows.** Five `PointLight`s, one behind each lit window at (window center x, window center y, 0.17), with `castShadow: false`, intensity 0.4 (relative to the phone reference of 1.0; absolute values wait on `mod-lights` calibration), distance 0.25 and decay 2. Color is the fluorescent tone, `lerp(PALETTE.cream, PALETTE.gator, 0.15)`, because PALETTE has no fluorescent entry (README §6). They exist only to make the tissue panes glow from behind.
- **Lobby spot** (the only shadow caster): a `SpotLight` at (0.14, 0.05, 0.30) aimed at (0.14, 0.00, 0.60), angle 0.7, penumbra 0.4, intensity 1.5, same fluorescent tone. It throws a trapezoid pool onto the plaza in front of the door. From 4.8 to 5.4 it backlights him and throws his silhouette toward the camera along the plaza.
- **Blob shadow:** there is no wall behind him, so nothing looms. From 0 to 4.8 the blob casts only a contact shadow on the plaza, **scale 1.0×**. From 4.8 to 5.75 its shadow streaks along the plaza next to his, **≤ 1.5×** its width. The loom starts inside, in s10. Outside, under high, far light, the shadow is small, which is consistent with the rule.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.5× the default. The moon is cream paper lit by ambient alone, with no glow of its own (doc 03 rule 7).
