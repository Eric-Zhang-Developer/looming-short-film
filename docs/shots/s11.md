## Light
- **0.0–3.6, key: the fluorescent over his table.** `fluorescent({ flickerSeed: seedOf('s11', 1) })`, a downward `RectAreaLight` 0.14 × 0.01 at (0.03, 0.21, 0.10), out of frame top, plus the module's dim `DirectionalLight` pointing straight down for the shadow. Intensity 0.6, fluorescent tone `lerp(PALETTE.cream, PALETTE.gator, 0.15)`. **Blob shadow: a pool on the table directly beneath it, scale 1.0× (≤ 1.2×).** The overhead light keeps it small, as the rule says.
- **Laptop screen** (1.5–5.0): a `PointLight` at the lid center, `PALETTE.cream`, `castShadow: false`, intensity 0.15, distance 0.08. It lights his snout pale. The screen itself shows cream with scribble glyphs, never text. At 5.0 it sleeps (steps to 0).
- **3.6 on, shadow key: the phone.** `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, distance: 0.6 })` at the phone, aimed with `aim(spot, blobCenter)`, intensity 0 → 1.0 over 2 frames at 3.6. Phone → blob is (+0.025, +0.006, −0.035) at 3.6 and (+0.025, +0.006, −0.026) from 4.4. **Blob shadow scale: 4.7× at 3.6 → 6.0× at 4.4 (eased), held at 6.0× to the end.** The shadow lies across the table top (grazing) and climbs the stacks behind it. At 6.0× its center is at (0.155, 0.074) on the wall and it is 0.144 m across. This is "closes over the table."
- **Face fill** (3.6 on): a `PointLight` at the phone, `PALETTE.phone`, `castShadow: false`, intensity 0.3, distance 0.06. His body is more than 60° off the phone spot's axis and casts nothing into it.
- **`tableLightDies` (4.2):** the table fluorescent flickers (seeded, 4.2–5.0) and dies to 0 by 5.0. From 5.0 the phone is the only light besides ambient.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.4× the default.
