## Light
- **Key: the fluorescent over the aisle (the Custodian's light).** `fluorescent({ flickerSeed: seedOf('s14', 1) })`, a downward `RectAreaLight` 0.14 × 0.01 at (0.02, 0.21, 0.26), above frame top, plus the module's dim `DirectionalLight` pointing straight down. Intensity 0.6, **steady: no flicker in this shot.** Fluorescent tone `lerp(PALETTE.cream, PALETTE.gator, 0.15)`. **Tiny blob shadow: a pool on the tray directly beneath it, scale 1.0× (≤ 1.1×).** The tiny blob sits under an overhead light, and its shadow is tiny.
- **The hero's phone**, unchanged from s12/s13: `phoneLight` at (0.005, 0.038, 0.150), aimed at the hero's blob, intensity **0.6** (still dimmed). **Hero blob shadow: 7.5×** on the stacks behind the table, center (0.19, 0.083). Face fill at 0.18. The phone's cone points toward −z, away from the aisle, so the cart and the Custodian never enter it.
- **The two shadows share the frame:** the hero's 7.5× shadow on the wall behind, and the Custodian's 1× pool on the cart in front. That contrast is the point of the shot (doc 03 rule 6: the shadow's direction shows which light is winning). Keep the aisle fluorescent's pool on the aisle floor (z 0.20–0.32); it must not wash out the hero's wall shadow (the wall stays ≤ 0.3× the brightness of the aisle floor).
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.3× the default. The hero's table fluorescent stays dead.
