# Shot s14 · The Custodian passes

**Time:** 1:35–1:40 (95–100 s, 5 s) · **Beats:** `custodianPass` @ 0.5, `tinyBlobSeen` @ 2.3 (from `src/timeline.js`)
**Story job:** Someone else's exam blob is tiny and asleep under an ordinary overhead light. He sees that it can be small. The Custodian's passing even wakes the light over the hero's desk for a moment, so his shadow drops and, for a moment, he sees his own blob as it is: big (2.5×), but nothing like its shadow; he doesn't move, and the light times out as the cart goes.

Conventions are in `docs/shots/README.md` (gator 0.080 m, hero blob 2.5×, r 0.030, `BLOB_R = { start: METER.gulp5 }`, boil rate 10).

## Light
- **Key: the fluorescent over the aisle (the Custodian's light).** `fluorescent({ flickerSeed: seedOf('s14', 1) })`: one dim `DirectionalLight` pointing down and tilted 15° toward camera, so the tiny blob's pool falls in front of it on the tray (README §3's smoke-paper finding). Intensity 0.6, **steady: no flicker in this shot.** Fluorescent tone (README §6). **Tiny blob shadow: a pool on the tray, scale 1.0× (≤ 1.1×).** The tiny blob sits under an overhead light, and its shadow is tiny. It's lit because he keeps moving through it. The `ceiling_header` flat keeps this light off the hero's desk, which has its own zone (below).
- **The hero's desk zone** (README §3): `sensorLight({ angle: 0.8, penumbra: 0.5 })` at (0.000, 0.127, 0.040), aimed at (0.000, 0, 0.098). Dead at the start; its trough is dull at the top of the frame.
  - **1.0, wake.** The cart is well into frame (its leading edge at x 0.115) and its movement trips his zone. The README §3 keys with t₀ = 1.0: strikes at 1.042, 1.167 and 1.292, settled from 1.375. The tube in frame glows.
  - **1.0–4.2: his blob sits in its own small pool, 1.31×,** in front of it on the desk. At `tinyBlobSeen` (2.3) both shadows are small in one frame, each blob under its own overhead light: the Custodian's 0.33× asleep, his own 2.5× awake and watching. The point is no longer that his blob is small; it's that its shadow is. He doesn't move: he only watches the cart.
  - **4.2, timeout.** The Custodian is past him (3.68) and leaving, and the hero has been still all along. `sensorTimeout` from 4.2 (s11's chatter and seed): dead by 5.0, the same local times as s11's death.
- **Rival (doc 03 rule 6, README §5): the hero's phone**, unchanged from s12/s13 (README §4): `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9 })` at (−0.010, 0.004, 0.114), aimed through the blob's centre at its wall hit (0.078, 0.078, 0), intensity **0.6** (still dimmed). It follows his zone (README §4): 0.2 on frames the zone is at ≥ 0.5, back to 0.6 on the dark frames and after the timeout. **Hero blob shadow: S 2.85 = 7.1 units** on the window wall behind the desk, centre (0.078, 0.078), at 0–1.0 and from 5.0, flashing back through the stutter and the chatter. Face fill at 0.18. The phone's cone points toward −z, away from the aisle, so the cart and the Custodian never enter it.
- **The two shadows share the frame.** Before the wake and after the timeout: the hero's 7.1-unit shadow (S 2.85 on the 2.5× blob) on the wall behind, and the Custodian's 1× pool on the cart in front (doc 03 rule 6: the shadow's direction shows which light is winning). Between them, for three seconds, both are small. The Custodian's light stays on because he keeps moving; the hero's dies because he doesn't. That is the point of the shot, and it teaches the sensor before s15. The near-vertical fluorescent grazes the wall (Lambert ≈ 0 on a plane parallel to the light), so the wall stays dark and the hero's shadow keeps its contrast (the wall stays ≤ 0.3× the brightness of the aisle floor).
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.3× the default.
