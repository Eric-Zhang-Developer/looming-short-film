# Shot s01 · Dawn skyline, push to the window

**Time:** 0:04–0:10 (4–10 s, 6 s) · **Beats:** none in `src/timeline.js` (shot has `beats: {}`) · **G4:** it now follows s00's notification, which cuts in on the dimmed phone; nothing in the shot changes.
**Story job:** It is early morning in a paper Gainesville, and this one window is where the story lives.

## Characters and action
No characters. The only motion is the camera push, the boil, and the light warming.
- 0.0–6.0: `oak_branch` sways ±0.6° about its top-left pivot, period 3.0 s, sampled on 2s (`onTwos`).
- 0.0–6.0: moss strands on `oak_branch` follow the sway with `spring` lag (k 6, w 18), on 2s.

## Light
- **Key: dawn.** `dawnLight()` placed behind the skyline at (−1.2, 0.5, −1.5), aimed at (0.15, 0.30, 0.60): elevation about 5°, so every cardstock layer is backlit and reads as silhouette with a lit torn rim (edge catch), and the tissue sky and window glow through. `update(t, k)` with k = kf(tLocal, [[0, 0.10], [6, 0.25]]): cold navy at the top, the first rose at the horizon.
- The skyline layers must not shadow the window: skyline tops (y ≤ 0.23) stay below the window (y 0.28–0.36) along the light direction. Check it in the 5.5 s still.
- **Fill:** `ambient()` only, at its default very low level. Facade front stays near Night Navy `#1E2440`.
- The window pane is the brightest thing in frame from 4.0 s on. Its glow comes only from translucency (tissue, `translucency` 0.8), not emissive.
- No blob in this shot, so no shadow-scale target.

## Camera
- `PerspectiveCamera`, FOV 20° vertical, 16:9.
- Start (0.0 s): position (0.00, 0.42, 2.50), looking at (0.00, 0.42, 0.00). Frame at the sky plate is 1.57 × 0.88 m; the 1.8 × 1.0 sky covers it with margin (checked: plate corners project outside the frame).
- End (6.0 s): position (0.17, 0.33, 1.00), looking at (0.18, 0.32, 0.60). The window fills 24% of frame width and 57% of frame height (checked by projection).
- Move: one slow push-in, position and look-at both `kf(tLocal, [[0, start], [6, end]], ease)`. Camera on 1s (real `t`). No other move.
- DOF: focus distance = distance from camera to the facade plane (z = 0.60), recomputed every frame, so the facade stays sharp and the skyline goes soft as the camera closes in. Default aperture.
