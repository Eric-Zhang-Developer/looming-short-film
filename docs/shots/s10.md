## Light
**Cause → effect, both in one frame: the overhead panel (top of frame) and the puddle under the blob (on the floor).** Before the threshold, a far, low light behind him (lamp 5) stretches the shadow long toward us; on the frame the blob crosses into the overhead light, the overhead light wins and the shadow snaps to a puddle. It's s06's lamp click, except he did it by walking.

- **0.00–1.46, key: lamp 5**, outside, behind him: a shadow-casting `SpotLight` at (−0.03, 0.100, −0.25) (s09c's lamp 5: same cool colour, `decay 0`), aimed at the blob. The blob is 0.30 from it along z, so its shadow lies **long on the ground toward the camera**, across the sill and through the doors (the tissue panes pass the light) onto the vestibule floor: a dark tongue with two eye holes, reaching toward the lens. **Through the overhead pool it's washed out**; it shows on the dark floor before the pool and beyond it. Re-solve: the tongue reaches the floor beyond the pool (z ≥ 0.22) and its eye holes land there.
- **The overhead light** (warm, `lerp(PALETTE.lamp, PALETTE.cream, 0.3)`: the same warm as Marston's windows in s09c): a `SpotLight` at the recessed panel (0.000, 0.142, 0.13), aimed straight down, `angle: 0.7, penumbra: 0.5`, shadow-casting, **on the whole shot** (the library's light stays on; doc 02). The panel glows (MeshBasic) at the top of frame. Before he enters, its pool lies on the floor just inside the door, empty.
- **1.50 `lightFlood`: the snap.** On this frame (the blob's landing inside the pool), **lamp 5's spot drops to 0.2** (README §4's out-shone rule, applied at the threshold) and the overhead spot is the only key. The blob's shadow becomes **a puddle directly under it, ≤ 1.3× its width** (pool scale 0.142 / (0.142 − 0.026) ≈ 1.22 at the blob's widest section, about its centre height), on the same frame. One frame, no fade: light on 1s. The long tongue on the floor is gone because the light that made it was out-shone, not because anything moved.
- **His face:** outside, a silhouette (lamp 5 behind him, ≤ 15% face fill). From 1.50, lit from above: the top of his head, brow ridge and snout warm, the underside of his jaw in soft shadow. **This is the reverse of s07–s09's under-lighting (phone, the nightstand lamp) and the film's first top-light on his face since s06's lamp.**
- **Close-up:** the same overhead key from above and slightly in front: warm on the brow, the closed lid, the snout's top; a soft shadow under the jaw. No rim from behind.
- **Character fill** (README §10): warm, only if the face measures under 30% of the lit floor, after 1.50.
- **Ambient:** 6% of s02's.
