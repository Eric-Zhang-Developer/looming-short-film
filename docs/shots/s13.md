## Light
**Light from above at every desk: small pools, small shadows. It's s11's light, repeated across the room.**
- The pendants are not drawn (they'd hang between this camera and the desks; the top view is schematic, README §8, as G4). Their light is: a warm pool decal on each occupied desk (`lerp(PALETTE.lamp, PALETTE.cream, 0.3)`, the v4 Marston warm, radial falloff to 0 at r 0.05, additive), so each working gator sits in their own light.
- **Shadow decals** (G4's mechanism, `blob.silhouette()`, `PALETTE.ink`, opacity 0.85, soft edge 0.004): **each is the blob's own silhouette at 1.1× (crowd) or 1.23× (hero), centred under the blob and offset 2 mm south** (toward the camera's bottom edge, as the pendants hang a little north of each desk's centre). No decal ever extends beyond its own desk. No growth, no wake: they're there from frame 0.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.6× (was 0.3×): the room is lit, not phone-dark. Plus one dim warm `DirectionalLight` from straight above, `castShadow` off, for the figures' paper.
- No phone glow anywhere. No fluorescent flicker.
