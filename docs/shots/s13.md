## Light
Doc 04 (Crowds): **each desk gets its own shadow decal, not its own light.** The hero uses a decal too, so that "everyone has one" reads as one system.

- **Phones** (key, "many phones"): each phone is a cutout from `props_top_sheet` at the desk's front corner, 0.03 from the desk center on the occupant's side. At its wake time its screen and a cyan radial glow decal (`PALETTE.phone`, radius 0.04 m on the desk top) step from 0 to full over 2 frames. Glow strength is 1.0 for the hero's phone (which carries s12's dim of 0.6) and 0.35 for every crowd phone: "a few faint phone glows."
- **Shadow decals** (`PALETTE.ink`, opacity 0.85, soft edge 0.006 m): the blob's own silhouette, eye holes included, generated from the blob field (code). Each starts under its blob and extends away from that desk's phone (its near edge at the blob center): **width = blob diameter × the desk's shadow scale, length = 1.5 × width** (a low light grazing a horizontal surface). For a crowd desk the decal grows from 1× to its target over wake → wake + 0.5 with `easeOut`, at full opacity from wake + 0.1: the shadow springs up when the phone lights. Before wake, a crowd desk has no shadow and no glow. The hero's phone is at (+0.030, −0.020), and his decal is at **7.5×** (0.18 × 0.27 m) from frame 0, pointing northwest (away from the phone) off his desk and onto the floor.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.3× the default, so the desks and figures read as dark shapes against a darker floor. There is no fluorescent light in this shot. The room is lit only by phones, which is why it appears desk by desk.
- **Lamps:** the hero's desk has the unlit `desk_lamp` (top view) at its northeast corner (+0.04, +0.02). No other desk has a lamp. They appear in s16 and s18, which are `sb-act3`'s call.

## Camera
- `fov` 20. Position (0, 0, 1.60), looking straight down −Z with up = +Y. **Locked.** No crane and no pull-back (doc 03 allows only push-ins and lateral parallax).
- Framing at the floor: x ±0.50, y ±0.28. The grid spans x −0.375 → +0.375 and y −0.163 → +0.163, so the hero's desk is dead center.
- DOF: focus 1.56 (the desk-top height), aperture 0.008, maxblur 0.004. It is nearly flat, because everything sits within 0.08 m of height.
- LUT `act2b`.
