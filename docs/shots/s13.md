## Light
**The rule, literal:** the phone (low, close) makes the big shadow, and the pendant overhead makes the small pool. Never a big shadow without a lit phone.
- **Pendants** (not drawn: they'd hang between camera and desks, README §8): a warm pool decal on every occupied desk, `lerp(lamp, cream, 0.3)`, additive. His is full from frame 0. The crowd's sit at 0.15 of full while their phone is up and come up to full over 0.25 s from their down. Each blob also has its pool shadow (silhouette × 0.127 / (0.127 − r), 2 mm south), always on.
- **Phones (v3's rig):** at the desk's front corner, 0.03 from the centre. On the wake, the screen and a cyan glow (strength 0.35) step on over 2 frames, and an unshadowed cyan point fill (0.14 cd, 0.2 m up) lights that desk and the floor round it, so the room appears desk by desk. On the down, the phone flips over 2 drawings to `phone_face_down`: the screen goes on the first drawing, and the glow and fill die over 0.25 s as it turns onto its face, in step with the loom.
- **Looms (v3's):** the blob's silhouette, from its centre, thrown away from the phone. They spring up from 1× over 0.5 s on `easeOut`, at full opacity (0.85) from wake + 0.1, and fall back to 1× over 0.25 s from the down, fading out with their phone's light, so no loom outlasts its light.
- **The room:** v3's dim cool light from straight above (`lerp(phone, cream, 0.5)`, 0.9) and ambient ×0.3 under the `marston` grade, warming to v6's pendant warm (0.55), ambient ×0.6 and the `amber` grade (s11's and s15's) from `phonesDown` to `homeChord` + 0.5. The grade is one 3D LUT re-mixed between the two in 1/24 steps.
