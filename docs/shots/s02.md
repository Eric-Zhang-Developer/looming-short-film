# Shot s02 · The ink drop is born

**Time:** 0:09–0:19 (9–19 s, 10 s) · **Beats:** `calendarZoom` @ 1.5 (global 0:10.5), `inkDropForms` @ 5.0 (global 0:14)
**Story job:** The camera pushes in to the calendar, where Friday says **"Calc 2 exam"**, circled in red. A drop of ink forms on that circle and becomes a small, curious creature that looks at the words it came from, then at him, and comes to him.
