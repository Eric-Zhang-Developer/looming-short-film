# Shot s02 · The ink drop is born

**Time:** 0:06–0:15 (9 s) · **Beats:** `inkDropForms` @ 4.0 (global 0:10)
**Story job:** A drop of ink forms on Friday's red circle, beside the exam glyph, and becomes a small, curious creature that looks at where it came from, then at him, and comes to him.
