# Shot s00 · The notification (cold open)

**Time:** 0:00–0:04 (0–4 s, 4 s) · **Beats:** `notifyBuzz` @ 0.5 (global 0:00.5)
**Story job:** Before we see anything else, the film says it plainly: he has a Calc 2 exam on Friday. A phone buzzes in a dark room and its screen says so (G4: "it should be a canvas notification or something"; doc 07: generic, no Canvas branding).

## Characters and action
No characters. The phone is the actor.
- 0.00–0.50: black. The screen is dark; the room is pre-dawn navy at its ambient floor. The audience hears the buzz before they see anything.
- **0.50 `notifyBuzz`:** the phone buzzes. On the buzz it jitters ±0.0004 in x on alternating frames for 0.33 s (on 1s, a real vibration, not on 2s), and the screen wakes in one frame to the notification.
- 0.50–2.00: hold on the banner. The badge pulses once (scale 1 → 1.1 → 1) over 0.75–1.00.
- 2.00: a second buzz (the same jitter, 0.33 s). Phones buzz twice; it also re-reads the line for anyone who missed it.
- 3.50–4.00: the screen dims to 40% (it times out), so the cut to s01's dawn sky is a cut from cold to warm.

## Light
- **Key: the phone screen** (the screen-light convention, README §4), here only as an emissive face plus the cyan face-fill `PointLight` at the phone + (0, 0.004, 0), `distance` 0.06. It lights the desk around the phone and the page's corner; there is no blob, so no shadow rule here. Intensity 0 before 0.50, full on the 0.50 frame, 0.4 from 3.50 to 4.00 (on 1s).
- **Fill:** `ambient()` at 15% of its s02 level (pre-dawn).
- The banner's red badge is the first red in the film; the calendar's circle in s02 answers it. Doc 03: red marks the exam.

## Camera
- FOV 20°. Looking straight down at the phone from 0.09 m above it: position (−0.010, 0.090, 0.116), lookAt (−0.010, 0.0015, 0.114), up vector −z so the phone's long side reads left to right. The phone fills about 60% of the frame width. **Locked.** Re-measure the framing at build so the banner's lettering is ≥ 40 px tall.
- DOF: focus on the screen; the page edge and the desk fall off.

## Sound
- **0.00–0.50:** near silence, the room tone at its lowest.
- **`notifyBuzz`:** `phoneBuzz` (new foley, doc 05): a short buzz, the phone rattling on wood, 0.33 s. A second `phoneBuzz` at `notifyBuzz` + 1.5. No chime (a chime would be a UI sound; the buzz is a physical one).
- No music. The Exam motif is saved for the drop in s02.
