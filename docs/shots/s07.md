# Shot s07 · Blackout blinds: each one feeds it

**Time:** 0:50–1:02 (12 s) · **Beats:** `blindsStart` @ 0 (global 0:50), `blind1` @ 6.0 (0:56), `gulp3` @ 6.5 (0:56.5), `blind2` @ 8.0 (0:58), `gulp4` @ 8.5 (0:58.5)
**Story job:** The morning sun glares on his game, so he puts up blackout blinds. Each blind he pulls down shuts out a band of daylight, and each time the blob gulps and the screen's shadow steps up one size, until only the screen's glow is left and the shadow fills the wall and ceiling. He shut out the passing day, and that is what fed it (G4: "I don't understand what's going on here. It doesn't tie into the story").

**G4 changes:** curtains → two roller blinds, night → morning (the day after s06; the sun is the motive), the phone → the laptop, and one gulp → two, one per blind (gulps 3 and 4, README §1). The room is `home_kit` at real scale (ceiling 0.13, windows 0.041–0.095). The screen light and the blob keep the blob-meter pass's positions, so S stays 10.0 and the peak stays 20 units.

## Characters and action
A time-lapse inside one locked shot. Each station opens with a **single-frame pose pop** (the stop-motion jump is the time cut), then plays pose to pose on 2s.

**Station 1, 0.00–2.00: blind A's brackets.** Standing on tiptoe under window A (the window head is at 0.097, just under his head top), drill raised to (−0.17, 0.097).
- 0.17–0.33: anticipation, leaning back 4°. 0.33–1.67: drilling: drill and arms jitter ±1°, alternating every 2s frame. Eyes `closed` (squinting into the sun), mouth `line`.
- 1.67: stops. 1.83: blows on the hole, mouth `o`.

**Station 2, 2.00–4.00: blind B.** Pop: under window B, both blinds' rollers in his raised hands.
- 2.17: anticipation crouch, −3 mm. 2.50: lift. **3.50: blind B's roller snaps into its brackets.** 3.67: steps back, mouth `smile`, both blinds rolled up. The drill is on the floor.

**4.00 pop: prone on the floor** with the laptop, gaming. Eyes `tired`, mouth `neutral`. Key taps at 4.33, 4.83, 5.17.
- 4.50: **the glare:** band B lies across the laptop. He squints (eyes `closed` one drawing), shades the screen with his far hand (one drawing, 4.67), gives up.
- **5.33: the look.** Eyes `side`, down-left at the blob sitting in the sun 4 cm from his screen, looking at him. **5.50: the look-away:** eyes up to the window.
- 5.67 pop: standing at window A, hand on the blind's bottom bar. 5.83: blind A half down (one drawing).
- **6.00 `blind1`:** blind A fully down. Band A is gone on this frame.
- 6.17 pop: prone again at the laptop. Key taps at 6.67, 7.17.
- **7.33: the look again,** at the blob, now the only sunlit thing in the room. **7.50: the look-away** to window B.
- 7.67 pop: standing at window B. 7.83: blind B half down.
- **8.00 `blind2`:** blind B fully down. The last of the sun is gone; only the screen's glow is left.
- 9.00 pop: prone at the laptop, lit cyan from below, tiny under what's on the wall. Key taps every 0.5 s from 9.33. He never looks up.

**The Exam,** at its contact point all shot. Wobble 1. Its eyes change at each pop, as time passes: station 1 `open` (watching him), station 2 `side`, from 4.0 `open` on him.
- 0.00–6.00: it sits in band B, bright, with a plain sun shadow lying along the floor toward camera, as wide as itself.

- **6.50 `gulp3`: the third gulp** (README §1), eyes on him. 6.417 squash 0.3, eyes `closed`; **6.50 snaps to 1.75× (r 0.021)**, stretched, eyes `open`; settles by 6.75. The wall shadow swells with it.

## Light
Keys switch on beats (README §5): the sun is the key while any band is in; the screen takes over at `blind2`.

- **The sun** (the apartment window rig, s04): `dawnLight()` behind the back wall, k 0.9 (morning gold), castShadow on, direction **elevation 13°, azimuth 13° toward +x**, so window B's floor patch runs z 0.178…0.411 and covers the blob's contact point and the laptop, and window A's patch lies to its left (x ≈ −0.08…−0.04 at z 0.38), on the rug (`evidence/spec-g4/geometry.txt`). The cardstock wall blocks it everywhere but the two windows. Each blind is an opaque plane that cuts its window's band on the frame it's down: A at 6.00, B at 8.00. From 8.00 the only daylight is the 1.5 mm side gaps, under 5% luminance (s08's seams).
- **The screen** at L (screen-light convention, README §4): `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, penumbra: 0.4, distance: 1.0 })` aimed along L → blob, plus the cyan face fill. **Its level steps with the daylight** (README §4's out-shone rule, applied to the sun): **0.2** while both bands are in (0.00–5.96), **0.5** with one (6.00–7.96), **1.0** with none (from 8.00). So the loom's contrast on the wall steps up with each blind: about 20%, about 50%, full.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 60% of its s02 level, 35% from 6.00, 15% from 8.00.
- **Blob shadow (receiver: back wall z = 0, then the ceiling):** S = 0.4222 / (0.4222 − 0.38) = **10.0** all shot (the screen and the contact point don't move).
