## Light
**One key: the phone, low and close under the blob.** This is the rule at its plainest: the light is below the thing, so the shadow goes up and over everything.
- **Key, the phone screen** at P: `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, penumbra: 0.4, distance: 1.0 })` aimed every frame with `aim(spot, …)` along P → the blob's centre (README §4's stylized screen light: aimed at the blob whatever way the screen faces). The blob sits up and to the right of the phone, so the ray from P through the blob rises toward +x and **lands on the ceiling** above the foot of the bed. Intensity constant (a faint ±3% seeded flicker per 2s drawing as the feed scrolls, so the shadow edge lives).
- **Shadow numbers (start geometry; re-solve at build):** P → blob centre is (+0.054, +0.032, −0.005) at 1.5× (centre ≈ 1.08 r above the contact). The ray meets the ceiling (y 0.145) about 3.1× that distance from P, so S ≈ 3.1 along the ray, and the shadow lies on the ceiling at a grazing angle of about 30°, stretched about 2× along x. At 1.5× that's a dark shape about 0.13 m across and 0.25 m long on the ceiling; **at 2× after the gulp, about 0.17 × 0.33 m: the whole ceiling band in frame.** Tune P's height (not the blob) until, after the gulp, the shadow spans ≥ 80% of the ceiling's visible width with both eye holes on the ceiling.
- **His face** is outside the cone: he's behind the phone (the ray runs away from him). Only the blob casts (the film's convention: every other piece has `castShadow` off), so his hands and knees throw nothing.
- **Face fill:** the cyan `PointLight` at P (README §4), `distance` 0.06, 20% of the spot: his snout and the underside of his jaw lit cyan from below, which is what "phone glow under his chin" looks like.
- **Moon:** the window panes glow faint moon-blue from behind (a dim `dawnLight` behind the wall at k 0, intensity tuned so the panes read at ~8% of the phone-lit ceiling). It casts nothing into the room. It's there so s09a has a light left when the phone goes out.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 5% of s02's level. The room is dark; the phone and its shadow are the picture.
- The clock and calendar sit outside the cone's core and catch only the faint spill: the clock reads (hands navy on cream) but dim. The red circle is a dark red ring.

## Sound
- **`bedRot` (@ 0):** the score **thins and flattens**: what's left of the Act 2 groove from s06 drops to one sustained, slightly flat felt-piano dyad that decays over the shot. Room tone at night.
- **Each thumb flick:** `scrollTick` (new, doc 05): a tiny dry tick, −22 dB, seeded per flick. These are the shot's rhythm: they stop for the glance and start again.
- **`clockGlance` (6.0):** nothing new; the flicks stop, and the silence is the note.
