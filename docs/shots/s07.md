## Characters and action
A time-lapse inside one locked shot. Each station opens with a **single-frame pose pop** (the stop-motion jump is the time cut), then plays pose to pose on 2s.

**Station 1, 0.00–2.00: left bracket.** Ladder at x −0.23. He stands on the top step (feet y 0.18), facing the wall (3/4 open), drill raised to (−0.23, 0.26).
- 0.17–0.33: anticipation, leaning back 4°. 0.33–1.67: drilling: drill and arms jitter ±1°, alternating every 2s frame. Eyes `closed` (squinting), mouth `line`.
- 1.67: stops. 1.83: blows on the hole, mouth `o`.

**Station 2, 2.00–4.00: right bracket.** Pop: ladder at x −0.07, same pose mirrored.
- 2.17–3.50: drilling as above. 3.67: bracket on. Eyes `closed`, mouth `smile` (content). The drill is set on the floor in this station's last pose.

**Station 3, 4.00–6.00: rod and panels.** Pop: ladder at x −0.15, rod with both panels in his raised hands.
- 4.17: anticipation crouch, −3 mm. 4.50: lift. 4.83: the rod seats on the brackets (snap). 5.17–5.83: tugs each panel straight. Mouth `smile`.

**Station 4, 6.00–8.00: closing.** Pop: on the floor in front of the window at (−0.15, 0, 0.06).
- 6.17–6.83: pulls the left panel closed (ease-in-out). 7.00–7.67: the right panel.
- 7.67: steps back 1 cm. 7.83: one satisfied nod (head −6°, then 0°). Eyes `open`.
- **8.00 `curtainsDone`:** the last gap closes. The room goes black.

**8.50 pop (hidden by the black):** prone on the floor at z 0.405, phone face-up at L. Eyes `tired`, mouth `neutral`. Thumb flicks at 9.00 and 9.50. He is lit from below by the phone and looks tiny under what's on the wall.

**The Exam.** At B all shot, wobble 1. Its eyes change at each pop, as time passes: station 1 `open` (watching him), station 2 `side`, stations 3–4 `tired` (it's late for it too).
- 8.50: eyes `open`.
- 9.00: eyes `side`, mirrored up: it looks up at its own enormous shadow, echoing its glance in s03.
- 9.50: eyes `worried`. Even the blob is unsettled by how big this got. Never menacing.

## Light
- **0.00–8.00, key: moonlight through the window** (the window rig from s04). `dawnLight()` behind the wall at (x_m, 0.8, −1.0), aimed at the window center (−0.15, 0.18, 0), k = 0 (cold night), castShadow on. Moon x per station: 0.30, 0.25, 0.20, 0.15 (time passing). It throws a window-shaped patch on the floor with corners at about x −0.41..−0.14, z 0.18..0.43, and backlights him on the ladder: his shadow lands in that patch. The blob (x −0.005) sits outside the patch, in the dark, so **there is no readable blob shadow during the montage.** That's the calm before the peak.
- 6.17–7.67: as the panels close, the patch narrows in step with them. On the 8.00 frame the window rig's intensity drops to 5%: the panels are shut and only the permanent seams remain. At 5% they are hairlines below 5% luminance, and they become s08's gold seams when the dawn rises.
- **8.00–8.50: black.** Key 0. `ambient()` drops from 40% to 15% of its s02 level on the 8.00 frame. Only the boil and the faintest room shapes remain.
- **8.50–10.00, key: phone** at L (phone-rig convention, s03): `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, penumbra: 0.4, distance: 1.0 })` aimed along L→B, plus the cyan face fill. Intensity 0 → full on the 8.50 frame.
- **Blob shadow scale: 20.0×** (receiver: back wall z = 0). Center (−0.100, 0.164), width 0.48 (x −0.34..0.14), height 0.48 (y −0.076..0.404). The part below y 0 runs along the floor from the blob's base to the wall. The part above y 0.30 continues onto the ceiling, which the top-of-blob ray reaches at z 0.104.
