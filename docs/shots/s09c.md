## The idea in one line
**The light rule, run as a heartbeat.** Overhead light makes the shadow small; far, low light makes it long. Walking under a row of lamps plays both, over and over, on the pulse. The audience should feel the rule in their chest before s10 pays it off for good.

## Light
**One shadow light at a time, always a lamp, and always in frame with the shadow it throws.** The cause (a lantern) and the effect (the blob's shadow on the path) share every frame.
- **The lamps:** each lamppost has (a) its lantern glowing (MeshBasic on the vellum lantern, cool white `lerp(PALETTE.cream, PALETTE.phone, 0.3)`: the street lamps are the night's cold light, Marston will be warm), (b) a non-casting `PointLight` at the lantern, decay 2, that lights the pool of path under it (the bright patch that says "lamp"), and (c) the shared shadow light below.
- **The shadow light (stylized, like README §4's screen light):** two shadow-casting `SpotLight`s, A and B, alternate lamps (A: lamps 1, 3, 5; B: 2, 4), each sitting at its lamp's light point (x_k, 0.100, 0.09), aimed straight down, `angle: 1.2, penumbra: 0.2, decay: 0` (constant, so a long shadow far from the lamp stays as dark as the puddle under it). **Only one is the key at a time**: the one whose lamp is nearest the blob. At each hand-over the two crossfade over 0.25 s centred on a pulse beat: **4.0** (1 → 2), **7.0** (2 → 3), **10.0** (3 → 4, on the blob's landing: the shadow swings to point back at him on the dissonance), **14.5** (4 → 5). After lamp 5 the key stays lamp 5 to the end.
- **Shadow numbers** (only the blob casts; the light at H = 0.100, the 2× blob's top at h ≈ 0.048): a ground shadow's far edge lies at **d × H / (H − h) ≈ 1.92 d** from the lamp's foot, where d is the blob's distance from the lamp along the path.
  - Under a lamp (d ≈ 0): a puddle, ≤ 1.3× the blob's width, right under it.
  - Mid-span (d ≈ 0.12): the far edge 0.23 from the lamp, 0.11 beyond the blob: **the shadow ≈ 3× the blob's width, stretched along the path.** That's the heartbeat: puddle → 3× → puddle, every lamp.
  - **The block** (blob at 0.650, lamp 4 at 0.780, d 0.13): the shadow's far edge at 0.78 − 0.25 = 0.53, back past his feet at 0.590 by 6 cm: **he is standing in its shadow**, and lamp 4's lantern is in frame beyond it.
  - Re-solve at build with `shadowScale`; if the mid-span shadow reads under 2.5×, lower H (never below his head top + 0.005) rather than cheating the spot.
- **Moon and ambient:** `ambient()` at 4%; the moon is paper lit by ambient (doc 03 rule 7). Between lamps the path is dark except where the key reaches.
- **Marston (from 16.0):** the `lit_window_strip` glows warm (`lerp(PALETTE.lamp, PALETTE.cream, 0.3)`, MeshBasic), the entrance brightest. A dim warm `DirectionalLight` from the building's direction, `castShadow` off, ≤ 20% of the lamp key at his position, puts a warm edge on his front from 17.0 as he faces it. It's a rival (doc 03 rule 6), not a key: it throws nothing. The cold lamps behind him, the warm building ahead: two temperatures, and he's walking from one to the other.
- **Character fill** (README §10): a dim cool fill on his face only where it measures under 30% of the lit path; off inside each lamp's pool.
