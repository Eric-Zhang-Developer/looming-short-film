# Shot s23 · The sting

**v9 (v9-ending, docs/notes/v9-beats.md §1): 3 s, global 3:44–3:47 (224–227), the film's last shot.** Beats: `stingBuzz` 0.3, `stingShadow` 0.8, `stingLookUp` 1.9, `stingFlip` 2.3, `stingLook` 2.5.

- **Set:** s18's room at night: s02's desk, s18's lamp (on; its constants imported from s18.ts), the empty chair at frame left. The laptop, page and calendar are put away (the calendar's X and "Final" are drawn in s18 itself; an un-crossed exam on the wall would undo the ending). The blob at s17's size (r 0.005) dozes at (0.026, 0, 0.0205); the phone lies face up and dark at the desk's right end (0.039, 0.026), its screen tipped 25° toward the lens so it reads from this low angle (flat, it was a sliver).
- **Camera:** s18's last framing (`PUSH`), pushing 45% of the way toward the blob and its wall over the 3 s, on 1s.
- **0.3:** the phone buzzes (it shivers on the desk for 0.25 s) and lights: the lock screen, then s16's Reel from 0.65. The blob wakes (`open`), and its eyes slide to it (`side`).
- **0.8:** its shadow springs up the wall: s13's loom (the blob's own silhouette, its eyes as holes), from 1× to the projection's S ≈ 4.6 over 0.5 s, placed by the point-light projection from the screen, up and left over the lamp. Only while the phone is lit.
- **1.9:** it looks up and back at its shadow (`side`, tipped toward it), then a beat with heavy lids (`tired`, standing in for `sad`; `SAD` in s23.ts is the one constant to swap after v9-blob merges), sagging.
- **2.3:** it tips onto the phone's edge with a squash and the phone flips face down (two drawings on edge, s13's): the light goes, and the shadow collapses to 1× in 0.25 s, back to a puddle at its foot.
- **2.5–3.0:** it looks at the camera (`open`), a slow blink (`tired`, `closed` held, `tired`, `open`). The film ends: cut to black. It never grows.
- **Sound:** s00's buzz (the same buffer, sample-identical), s16's Reel clip `DepVdpeJdCN` from the same in-point (1.05 s, its build) from 0.65 through the phone band, cut dead on the flip; s16's face-down card tap on the flip; room tone and paper boil under it all.
