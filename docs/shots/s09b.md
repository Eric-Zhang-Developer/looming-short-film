## Light
**Cause → effect, all in the poster frame: the night-light (in frame, low at the left), the blob (in front of it), and the blob's shadow (filling the hall ahead), in that order down the hall.** The door's backlight is behind him, so the shadow goes ahead of him, and he walks into it.
- **Key: the night-light**, `night_light` at L = (−0.049, 0.020, 0.41): a `SpotLight` (the film's low-light form, like `phoneLight`), colour pale cool (`lerp(PALETTE.phone, PALETTE.cream, 0.5)`), `angle: 1.0, penumbra: 0.4, decay: 1`, aimed every frame at the blob's centre (README §4's stylized aim). Decay 1, not the physical 2: the hall still darkens toward the far end (doc 03 rule 3), but the end wall keeps enough light for the eye holes to read (≥ 2× the shadow's luminance; tune intensity, not decay, if they don't). Shadow-casting; only the blob casts (his `castShadow` stays off, the film's convention), so there is one shadow and it's the blob's.
- **Before the door opens (0.0–1.0):** the spot is aimed down the empty hall; the end wall is lit faintly and evenly.
- **On `doorOpen`:** the glow spills through the opening onto him (a fill from the low front-left; it lights his feet and hoodie, not his face from above).
- **The shadow** (blob landed at B = (−0.015, 0, 0.33), 0.08 in front of L along z): **S ≈ 5.1** on the end wall (0.41 / 0.08). The blob's 2× field (≈ 0.055 wide, 0.048 tall) throws a shape about **0.28 m wide**: wider than the hall, so it covers the whole end wall and the front door and runs up both side walls at the far end. The light is at 0.020, about the blob's mid-height, so the eye holes land at about y 0.05 on the end wall: **on the front door.** Below them the shadow lies along the hall floor from the blob's foot to the end wall: a dark road. Re-solve with `shadowScale` at build; tune L's height (± 0.005) so both eye holes sit on the door, and L's z so the hole spacing (≈ ±0.036) stays inside the door's width.
- **The poster frame's read:** a small lit gator at the bottom of the frame, a low glow beside him, a dark shape with two eyes filling the far end of the hall where the exit is.
- **When he steps in (4.50–6.00):** he walks through the shadow volume (it widens from the blob toward the end wall), so the darkness climbs his body from the feet up: by 5.5 everything below his shoulders is in shadow. This is "he walks into it", made literal by the light.
- **Close-up C5:** the night-light from behind-left and below: a rim on the back of his head, the jaw's underside, the top edge of his snout. His face is in his own half-shadow. Ahead of him (screen right) the frame is dark.
- **Ambient:** 3% of s02's. The hall is lit only by the night-light.
