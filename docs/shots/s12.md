# Shot s12 · Quiet 1

**Time:** 1:18–1:28 (78–88 s, 10 s) · **Beats:** `quiet1Start` @ 0 (from `src/timeline.js`)
**Story job:** The low point. He has given up for the night, and the shadow owns the frame.

**Quiet shot: fully locked.** The camera never moves, and no character changes pose. Only light, the blob's breathing shadow and the edge boil move; whether the breathing may stay is director question Q1. Boil rate is **4 per second** (`boilSeed(t, 4)`).

**The same set as s11, every position unchanged** (the Marston hero desk unit, `docs/shots/README.md` §2: window wall at z = 0, desk top y = 0, chair, backpack, page and pen, his zone's trough just above frame, the laptop at z 0.078, and the blob at its Marston mark, contact (0.0207, 0, 0.074), **2.5× (r 0.030)**, `BLOB_R = { start: METER.gulp5 }`). It sits at the peak, eyes level with his closed ones. Differences from the end of s11:

## Characters and action
**Student**: from `quiet1Start` (0.0) to the end, one pose, `head_down`, held for all 10 s with no motion at all. Hip (−0.075, −0.012). Lean +35°. Arms folded on the desk (both arms at 85° to the body). Head resting on the forearms at about x −0.040 with its underside at y 0.008: head angle −20°, snout pointing toward the phone. Eyes `closed`, mouth `neutral`.

**Desaturation** (doc 03, "drain on the low point"): the gator's paper saturation goes from 1.0 to 0.55 over 0.0–2.0 with `ease` and holds at 0.55. The blob and its shadow are not desaturated; the shadow stays the richest black in the frame.

**Blob:** eyes `open` for the whole shot, looking at him, with no blink. `wobble: 0.5`. **Breathing** (Q1 a): the blob mesh scales by `1 + 0.06·sin(2π·t/5)`, two slow breaths in 10 s with peaks at 1.25 and 6.25. A breath, not growth: it returns to 2.5× each cycle (README §1, no slow morphing).

## Light
- **Key: the phone, faint** (README §4). `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, penumbra: 0.4, distance: 0.6 })` at (−0.010, 0.004, 0.114), aimed through the blob's centre at its wall hit (0.078, 0.078, 0). **Blob shadow: S 2.85 (0.114 / 0.040) on the 2.5× blob = 7.1 units**, constant: the same size s15 opens on, and the spec's. This puts the spot back at the phone: anim-s12's z 0.1029 was solved for the old blob z 0.0884. With the breathing, the shadow is 0.183–0.207 m across. It is centred (0.078, 0.078) on the window wall, spans x −0.020..0.175, and runs off the top of the frame.
- His head stays outside the 52° cone (69° for the old blob; re-measure). The blob's half-angle is 38.7°.
- **Auto-dim** (the only light change): spot intensity is 1.0 from 0.0 to 6.0, then eases down to 0.6 over 6.0–6.5 and holds. **The frame from 6.5 to 10.0 is the darkest in the film.** The shadow gets fainter at the edges, not smaller.
- **Face fill:** a `PointLight` at the phone, `PALETTE.phone`, `castShadow: false`, distance 0.06. Intensity 0.30, easing to 0.18 over 6.0–6.5 with the dim. It is the cyan on his closed eye and snout.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.15× the default. Everything outside the phone's cone reads as near-black navy.
- No zone, laptop or room light: his zone timed out in s11 and he hasn't moved since.

## Camera
- `fov` 20. Position (0.040, 0.038, 0.440), looking straight down −Z. **Locked for all 10 s.**
- Framing at the wall: x −0.098 → 0.178, y −0.040 → 0.116. Projected at 1920×1080: his head (291, 791), the phone (490, 883), the blob's centre (799, 607), the wall-shadow centre (1221, 261), its left edge (543, 261) and right edge (1899, 261). The shadow spans the middle and right of the frame and runs off the top; its right edge sits about 60 px inside the frame at the breath's trough and just outside at its peak, which is fine (the left edge carries the breath).

- DOF: focus 0.342 (on the blob), aperture 0.03, maxblur 0.012. His head and the wall are slightly soft.
- LUT `act2b`.

## Sound
