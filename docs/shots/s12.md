# Shot s12 · Quiet 1

**Time:** 1:18–1:28 (78–88 s, 10 s) · **Beats:** `quiet1Start` @ 0 (from `src/timeline.js`)
**Story job:** The low point. He has given up for the night, and the shadow owns the frame.

**Quiet shot: fully locked.** The camera never moves, and no character changes pose. Only light, the blob's breathing shadow (see Gaps in the done note) and the edge boil move. Boil rate is **4 per second** (`boilSeed(t, 4)`).

## Set and layers
**The same set as s11, with every position unchanged** (stacks at z = 0, table top y = 0.032 spanning z 0.04–0.16, chair, backpack, unlit `desk_lamp` at x = 0.15, blob at (0.030, 0.044, 0.13)). Differences from the end of s11:

| z (m) | Layer | State |
|---|---|---|
| 0.10 | `laptop` | Lid open at 100°, screen **off** (dark navy) |
| 0.15 | `phone` | Propped face-up against his slack fingers at (0.005, 0.038, 0.150), screen tilted toward the blob and the wall |
| — | Table fluorescent | Dead (0), as it was from 5.0 in s11 |

## Characters and action
**Student**: from `quiet1Start` (0.0) to the end, one pose, `head_down`, held for all 10 s with no motion at all. Root (−0.050, 0.020, 0.10). Body leaned +35°. Arms folded on the table (arm_r and arm_l at 85°). Head resting on the forearms at about x = −0.015, with its underside at y = 0.040: head angle −20°, snout pointing toward the phone. Eyes `closed`, mouth `neutral`.

**Desaturation** (doc 03, "drain on the low point"): the gator's paper saturation goes from 1.0 to 0.55 over 0.0–2.0 with `ease` and holds at 0.55. The blob and its shadow are not desaturated; the shadow stays the richest black in the frame.

**Blob:** eyes `open` for the whole shot, looking at him, with no blink. `wobble: 0.5`. **Breathing:** the blob mesh scales by `1 + 0.06·sin(2π·t/5)`, two slow breaths in 10 s with peaks at 1.25 and 6.25.

## Light
- **Key: the phone, faint.** `phoneLight({ angle: 0.9, distance: 0.6 })` at (0.005, 0.038, 0.150), aimed with `aim(spot, blobCenter)`. Phone → blob is (+0.025, +0.006, −0.020). **Blob shadow scale: 7.5×** (0.150 / 0.020), constant. With the breathing, the shadow is 0.169–0.191 m across. Its center is at (0.19, 0.083) on the stacks, and it covers the right two-thirds of the frame above the table line.
- **Auto-dim** (the only light change): spot intensity is 1.0 from 0.0 to 6.0, then eases down to 0.6 over 6.0–6.5 and holds. **The frame from 6.5 to 10.0 is the darkest in the film.** The shadow gets fainter at the edges, not smaller.
- **Face fill:** a `PointLight` at the phone, `PALETTE.phone`, `castShadow: false`, distance 0.06. Intensity 0.30, easing to 0.18 over 6.0–6.5 with the dim. It is the cyan on his closed eye and snout.
- **Ambient:** `ambient()` at 0.15× the default. Everything outside the phone's cone reads as near-black navy.
- No fluorescent, laptop or lamp light.

## Camera
- `fov` 20. Position (0.09, 0.070, 0.50). Looks straight down −Z. **Locked for all 10 s.**
- Framing at the table plane (z = 0.13, 0.37 away): x −0.026 → 0.206. His head is at the left edge (about 7% across), the phone glow at about 15% and the blob at about 24%. On the wall (0.50 away), the frame is x −0.067 → 0.247 and y −0.018 → 0.158. The shadow runs from about 54% across to past the right edge.
- This is a **tighter** setup than the end of s11 (about 35% narrower at the table), so the cut doesn't read as a jump cut.
- DOF: focus 0.37 (on the blob), aperture 0.03, maxblur 0.012. His head and the wall are slightly soft.
- LUT `act2b`.

## Sound
- **`quiet1Start` @ 0: silence.** The music stem is at 0 for the whole 10 s (doc 05).
- Ambience: the distant fluorescent hum bed (−6 dB, continuing from s11), a very faint phone glow hum and the paper-boil bed.
- **The shadow breathing:** `subSwell({ hz: 45 })` with its amplitude following the blob's breath (period 5 s, peaks at 1.25 and 6.25). It should be felt more than heard. After 6.0 the sub stays at the same level; only the light dims.
