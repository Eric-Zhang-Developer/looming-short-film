## Sound
- From 0.0: the avoidance groove (all layers so far), the `subSwell` at its s05 level, and the phone glow hum.
- **`lampClickHome`:** `lampClick`, the film's master click: a dry plastic click plus the filament "tink". The groove, the sub and the phone hum all **cut dead on the click.**
- Just after the click: a tiny Exam motif on music box, softer than in s02 (doc 05: softer each time the blob is small). `snd-score` places it so it ends before the off click.
- **`lampOffHome`:** `lampClick`, **the identical buffer** (same seed and gain) as `lampClickHome`. Then 0.17 s of silence.
- `lampOffHome` + 0.17 s: the phone hum returns, and the `subSwell` springs back with a soft `paperRustle`. The groove re-enters on the next downbeat.
- The `+` offsets wait for a `CUES` offset field (README §9).
