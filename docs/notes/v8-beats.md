# v8 beat sheet (2026-10-20): approved by the director ("Now I want you to do version 8"); as built, see the note at the end

Director, after watching v7 with sound: the first half is much stronger and the humor works ("really fucking funny"); **the second half is weak on the moral**. "I procrastinated until 3 am, went to the library, locked in, got a good grade" isn't a heroic story. He asked for a scene where "I look at a practice problem … I get scared, so then I start scrolling" or gaming. Runtime may grow to **3:30**.

## The diagnosis
The only hard choice is s09a (1:10, 6 s). From s10 on (1:42) nothing pushes back: every beat after the threshold is a reward. The film never shows *why* he avoids, so the dodging reads as laziness and the library reads as a cure.

## The fix: the same flinch, twice, with two different answers
- **Act 1 (s03):** he opens the practice problem, ∫x·eˣ dx. The countdown pops over it, he flinches and **alt-tabs to the game**. Now the fear is about the work, not a word on a calendar.
- **Act 4 (s16):** the same integral in the library. He makes the sign error, crosses it out, flinches the same way, and **reaches for his phone**. The phone's low, close light throws the blob's shadow up the library wall: the film's own rule says the dread comes back the moment he holds the escape up to his face. He sees it and **turns the phone face down himself**. Then he solves it.
- **The moral:** the flinch doesn't go away; he stops obeying it. Walking into the light got him there, but staying is the real choice.
- **The ending (s18) shows what changed:** the next exam's drop, a glance at the laptop (the flinch, half a second), then the pen.

The cut list already set this up: at home the avoidance is the laptop game, and "the phone stays for Marston". He flees to the game at home and is tempted by the phone in the library.

## Rules this keeps
- **Gulps only grow it, solves only shrink it.** The s03 flinch takes over the existing `gulp1` (same time, 4.5), so Act 1's ladder is unchanged. The s16 relapse is one new gulp (2× → 2.5×). The first solve then takes it 2.5× → 1.5×, the biggest single shrink in the film, as the reward for staying.
- **The light rule:** the phone is a low, close light, so its shadow looms even under Marston's top light. Face down, only the pendant lights him and the shadow is a puddle again. No new rule.
- **The hero takes the step himself:** nothing interrupts the relapse. No buzz pulls him in and no one stops him. He reaches for the phone himself, and he puts it down himself.
- **The math is real:** the same ∫x·eˣ dx on the laptop in s03 and on paper in s16. The answer is xeˣ − eˣ + C, and the sign error is the one already crossed out.
